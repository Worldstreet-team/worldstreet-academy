**WORLDSTREET MASTERY ACADEMY (WMA)**
**The Academy of the New Economy**

## 1. The big idea

WorldStreet Mastery Academy (WMA) is envisioned as a globally positioned institution for skills, innovation, and wealth liquefaction—the Academy of the New Economy.

Its central purpose is simple:

> To equip people with knowledge that has utility, develop that knowledge into mastery through structured learning and application, and help them use their mastery to transform their personal financial capacity.

This Academy is not simply an online course platform. It is designed to function as a modern institution of learning, combining: structured learning programs, live cohort-based learning, practical skills development, faculty members and expert instructors, mentorship, assignments and assessments, projects and application, communities, certifications, career and income pathways AND a digital marketplace for additional knowledge products.

Our philosophy is simple: **Wealth Liquefaction:** Knowledge → Application → Mastery → Skills → Economic Value → Financial Transformation

## 2. The positioning

- **Official Name:** WorldStreet Mastery Academy
- **Primary Descriptor:** The Academy of the New Economy
- **Institutional Positioning:** The world's premier institution for skills, innovation and wealth liquefaction.

**Wealth Liquefaction**

Wealth Liquefaction is the process of transforming knowledge, skills, ideas, assets, opportunities and innovation into economic value and ultimately into financial capacity.

This gives us our own intellectual territory. Instead of simply saying, "We teach people skills," WMA says:

> We teach people how to turn knowledge into economic value.

## 3. The vision

To build the world's most impactful institution for developing people who can create, capture and multiply economic value in the new economy.

## 4. The mission

To equip individuals with relevant knowledge, practical skills, innovative thinking and access to economic opportunities that enable them to transform their personal economy.

The Academy therefore exists to close three major gaps:

1. **The Knowledge Gap** — People don't know what they need to know.
2. **The Mastery Gap** — People may know something but cannot apply it effectively.
3. **The Opportunity Gap** — People may have knowledge and skills but lack pathways to monetize them.

## 5. The WMA transformation model — the 5-stage Mastery Journey

- **Stage 1 — DISCOVER.** The learner discovers a field, skill or economic opportunity.
- **Stage 2 — LEARN.** The learner acquires foundational knowledge.
- **Stage 3 — MASTER.** The learner develops competence through structured practice, mentorship and assessment.
- **Stage 4 — APPLY.** The learner applies the knowledge to real-world problems, projects or opportunities.
- **Stage 5 — LIQUIDATE.** The learner learns how to convert the acquired capability into economic value. This is where the concept of wealth liquefaction becomes central.

Examples: Learn graphic design → master graphic design → build portfolio → acquire clients → generate income. Or learn AI → build AI solutions → solve business problems → create products/services → generate economic value.

## 6. The Academy has three major worlds

**WORLD 1 — THE ACADEMY.** This is the institutional learning environment. Schools, Programs, Faculty, Cohorts, Live classes, Assessments, Assignments, Mentorship, Certifications, Student communities and Projects. This is the premium, structured academic side.

**WORLD 2 — THE ACADEMY MARKETPLACE.** This is the open knowledge marketplace. Think Udemy + Coursera Marketplace + WMA ecosystem. External educators, experts and creators can submit courses. They do not automatically become WMA faculty. Important distinction: WMA Faculty ≠ Marketplace Instructor.

## 8. Every school should have a clear structure

When someone clicks a School, they should see: School Overview · What you'll learn · Who it's for · Career/economic applications · Available programs · Faculty · Upcoming cohorts · Learning pathways. Then the available programs, followed by enrollment options.

## 9. Programs and plans

- **Program** = What are you learning? Example: Crypto Trading Mastery.
- **Plan** = How do you want to learn it? Example: Basic / Standard / Executive.

**BASIC — Foundation.** Typical duration: 4 weeks. Includes foundation lessons, core concepts, selected recorded classes, learning materials, student community, basic assignments, basic assessments, student dashboard and certificate of completion where applicable. Pricing: Regular $300; discounted offer $49.

**STANDARD — Mastery.** Typical duration: 8–12 weeks. Includes everything in Basic plus beginner-to-advanced curriculum, full live cohort, practical assignments, projects, instructor interaction, assessments, mentorship, advanced resources and community. Example pricing: fee $1,000; discounted offer $199.

**EXECUTIVE — Personalized Mastery.** Designed for busy professionals and learners who want individualized learning. Includes everything in Standard plus a dedicated/private tutor, one-on-one sessions, personalized learning plan, flexible scheduling, individual feedback, private mentorship, progress reviews, personalized projects, executive community and premium support. Example pricing: fee $10,000; discounted offer $999.

## 10. The student journey

Visitor enters WMA → Understands the mission → Explores Schools → Chooses a Program → Reviews the three plans → Selects a plan → Completes payment → Payment is confirmed → Student receives welcome email + login credentials → Student enters the WMA Student Portal.

## 11. The WMA Student Portal

The dashboard should immediately answer: Where am I? What do I need to do? How much have I completed? What happens next?

Welcome section · Course progress · Next action / next class, inbox, messages · My Courses · My Classes · Recordings · Assignments · Assessments · My Points · Communities

## 12. The WMA Mastery Score

Every student can have a measurable WMA Mastery Score based on course completion, assessments, assignments, projects, attendance and practical application. This makes "mastery" measurable.

## 13. Certification

WMA Certificate of Completion · WMA Certified Practitioner · WMA Mastery Certification

Credentials should correspond to published academic requirements and demonstrated competency.

## 14. The community layer

Program Community · School Community · Academy Community. This creates relationships beyond classes.

## 15. The WMA Partners Program

Students can receive a unique WMA Partner Code, share it with others and earn according to the Academy's partner commission structure. The system can provide a partner dashboard, unique link/code, click tracking, enrollments, revenue and commissions.

## 16. The faculty system

People interested in becoming instructors apply through **BECOME A WMA INSTRUCTOR**: Application → Academic review → Interview → Teaching demonstration where appropriate → Approval → Onboarding → Faculty credentials → Teaching standards → Curriculum guidelines → Platform training.

**Faculty Dashboard:** Today's classes · Upcoming classes · My Students · Assignments · Course Materials · Live Classroom · Student Performance · Earnings · WMA Partners

## 17. The Academy Marketplace

The Marketplace is the open knowledge-commerce layer. Approved external creators can offer courses, masterclasses, workshops, templates and guides. Marketplace creators remain distinct from official WMA faculty.

**Marketplace Quality Control:** Application → Content review → Quality review → Accuracy review where relevant → Technical review → Approval → Publishing

WorldStreet Mastery Academy is a global institution for the new economy that equips people with practical knowledge, develops that knowledge into mastery, and helps them apply valuable skills to create economic value and transform their financial capacity.
