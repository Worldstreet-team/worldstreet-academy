## 15. FAQ

- **Who can join Worldstreet Mastery Academy?** Anyone who wants to develop practical knowledge and skills can explore our programs, subject to the requirements of individual courses.
- **Do I need previous experience?** Many programs are designed for beginners. Individual program pages will specify prerequisites where necessary.
- **Are classes online?** Programs can be delivered through the worldstreet academy LMS using the format specified on each program page.
- **Can I learn more than one course?** Yes. Students can enrol in multiple programs where available.
- **Do I receive a certificate?** Eligible programs may provide certificates upon meeting their completion requirements.
- **Can I pay online?** Yes. The platform should provide secure payment options available to the student's country.
- **What happens after payment?** Your enrollment is confirmed and your course access becomes available according to the program's delivery schedule.

## 18. Implementation Note

The website should not place all information on one giant page. The recommended experience
is a premium education marketplace: **Homepage → School → Program → Package → Checkout**.

This creates a clean conversion path and allows a visitor interested in a specific
program—such as Forex—to move naturally from "What is this?" → "What will I learn?" →
"Which package is right for me?" → "Pay."
